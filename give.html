    Questions

    What's the difference between backing the row and giving to charity?

    Backing the row (sponsoring a kilometre, a meal, a shift and so on) pays for the crossing itself: the boat, kit, food, race entry and training. Giving to charity goes only to our four charities. The two are kept in separate pots and never mixed.

    Does my charity donation pay for the row?

    No. Not a penny of a charity donation is spent on the row. The row is funded by us, our business sponsors and people who choose "Back the row".

    Is backing the row a charity donation?

    No. Project Persist isn't a registered charity, so money for the row is a contribution to our expedition costs. It isn't tax-deductible and Gift Aid can't be added. If you want your money to go to charity, choose "Give to charity".

    How is a charity donation split?

    Equally. Each of Action for M.E., Diabetes UK, Kom op tegen Kanker and Football Academy Noah receives 25% of what you give. If you tick "cover the card fee", they receive 25% of your full chosen amount.

    Is it secure?

    Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card payments for millions of businesses worldwide. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our website.

    Can I claim Gift Aid?

    Not on this page. Charity donations come to Project Persist first and are then passed on, and money for the row isn't a charity donation at all. If Gift Aid matters to you, you can give to Action for M.E. or Diabetes UK directly through their own websites.

    When do the charities receive the money?

    We pass donations on to each charity in regular instalments and will post every transfer in the Log on our main site, so you can see exactly where it went.

    I'd like to give as a business

    Brilliant. Have a look at our sponsorship packages, which include your logo on the boat, or get in touch.
