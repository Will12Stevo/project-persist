    Questions

    What's the difference between the two?

    Backing the row pays for the crossing: boat, kit, food and race entry. Giving to charity goes to our four charities. They're kept in separate pots.

    Does my charity donation pay for the row?

    No. The row is paid for by us, our sponsors and people who back the row.

    Is backing the row a charity donation?

    No. We're not a registered charity, so it isn't tax-deductible and Gift Aid can't be added.

    How is a charity donation split?

    Equally: 25% each to Action for M.E., Diabetes UK, Kom op tegen Kanker and Football Academy Noah.

    Is it secure?

    Yes. Payments go through Stripe, and your card details never touch our site.

    Can I claim Gift Aid?

    Not here. If Gift Aid matters to you, give to Action for M.E. or Diabetes UK directly.

    When do the charities get the money?

    We send it in instalments and post every transfer in the Log.
